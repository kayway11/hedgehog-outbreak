📖 How to Play

🎯 The Goal

One player is secretly Infected. Survivors complete missions and escape. The Infected spreads the virus.

🕹️ Controls

Move: left joystick or WASD
Look: drag the right side of the screen, or move your mouse
Sprint: hold Shift (desktop)

👥 Players

4–10 players. Join by room code, or find public rooms.

🦠 Infection

Infected hedgehogs infect others by touching them. Newly infected become hunters too.

🎯 Missions

5 mission stations. Complete them to fill the escape bar to 100%. Reach it → Survivors win.

🗳️ Meetings & Voting

Report a body or use an Emergency Button to gather everyone. Discuss, then vote. Majority vote ejects.

🏆 Winning

Survivors win if the escape bar fills, time runs out with survivors alive, or the Infected is voted out.
Infected wins if all Survivors are infected.

⏱️ Rounds

6-minute rounds. Scores accumulate across rounds.
